Research Profile

I work on a U.S. Department of Energy–funded project on multiphysics machine learning for subsurface energy systems. My research combines numerical methods for partial differential equations with scientific machine learning, focusing on nonsmooth solutions, discontinuous coefficients, and heterogeneous multiphysics systems. During my Ph.D., I studied stationary and time-dependent obstacle formulations for ice-sheet dynamics and developed feedforward and physics-informed neural solvers, work that led to DG-FEONet, a data-free neural operator based on SIPG discretization. My current research develops sparse reduced-order models and mesh-free solvers for geothermal and porous-media systems, and I also study hybrid quantum-classical finite element solvers for heterogeneous Darcy flow.
